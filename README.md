## About

I'm **Adrian Guichard**, a self-taught **full-stack JavaScript/TypeScript developer** based in Montpellier, France.

I like building the whole product rather than stopping at one layer: **interface, backend, data, architecture, testing, security, CI, deployment and production reliability**.

My work sits mainly at the intersection of **product engineering**, **interactive systems**, **3D / realtime experiences**, and **developer tooling / automation**. I care about software that is useful, technically solid, and still understandable as it grows.

## What I build

**Product engineering**  
Complete web products and SaaS-style applications, from user flows and interfaces to APIs, persistence and production delivery.

**Interactive systems**  
Realtime interfaces, browser games, simulations, Three.js / WebGL experiences and systems where state, motion and interaction matter.

**Tooling & automation**  
Developer tools, AI-assisted workflows, monitoring, infrastructure automation and internal systems that reduce repetitive work.

## How I work

- **Architecture before complexity** — clear boundaries, explicit responsibilities and reusable modules.
- **End-to-end ownership** — I prefer understanding how the complete system behaves, not only the layer I am editing.
- **Reliability before “done”** — tests, CI gates, smoke checks and failure paths are part of the feature.
- **Security by default** — least privilege, secret scanning, dependency review and defensive validation.
- **Pragmatic engineering** — use the simplest structure that can stay clear as the product grows.
- **Production-minded delivery** — deployment, monitoring, recovery and maintainability matter as much as the initial implementation.

## Current toolkit

- **Backend & realtime** — Node.js · Express · FastAPI · REST · WebSocket · Nakama
- **Data** — PostgreSQL · MongoDB
- **Quality** — Playwright · Vitest / Jest · ESLint · Prettier · Gitleaks

## Direction

I'm continuing to push deeper into **backend architecture, realtime systems, automation, AI tooling and production hardening**, while keeping the strong frontend and interactive side that got me into development in the first place. The goal is to build increasingly ambitious systems without losing clarity, reliability or control of the full product lifecycle.
